## Rating and credit rules

Evidence quality E (40%), impact I (35%), risk-adjusted feasibility F (25%) are each 1–100. **Rating = round(100 × (E/100)^0.40 × (I/100)^0.35 × (F/100)^0.25)**. The weighted geometric mean implements the requested evidence × impact × feasibility relationship and penalizes a weak factor. E reflects directly verified code and corroboration; I reflects correctness, user latency, memory or maintenance scope; F discounts lifecycle, concurrency, invalidation and semantic risks. Ratings are prioritization judgments, not predicted seconds saved.

Each shipped merged task has **one total point**. A complete unique good proposal earns 1; n complete equivalent proposals earn 1/n each; rejected work earns 0. For unequal completeness, divide the task into the explicit equal-weight scope units shown below, and apply 1/n among qualifying proposals covering each unit, then average the units. Thus full coverage receives more than partial coverage without a first-list bonus. All listed proposers remain in provenance, including rejected recipes with zero credit. Credit is computed per source submission and then summed by agent family; two submissions by the same family both count toward its submitted-task denominator.

Quality-weighted points = Σ(credit × merged rating/100). Normalized points and normalized quality = respective points / raw tasks submitted. Bundled source work orders with rejected subparts receive credit only for retained scope; their omission is recorded below. Related but distinct capabilities merged into one task share that task's one-point budget by scope, rather than each receiving a full point.
